## Reference sites to know
- **Merck Veterinary Manual** — merckvetmanual.com — the free standard reference; go here when the lesson is thin
- **AAVSB** — aavsb.org — the VTNE itself: content outline, candidate handbook, dates
- **AVMA** — avma.org — policies, program accreditation (CVTEA), the Model Veterinary Practice Act
- **NAVTA** — navta.net — the technicians' association, VTS specialties
- **VCA pet health library** — vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet — plain-English explanations of conditions, good for a first pass before the textbook
