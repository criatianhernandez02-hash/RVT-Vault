# Pain Management and Analgesia MOC

**VTNE weight: 7 %.** Closely tied to [[Anesthesia MOC]] and [[Pharmacy and Pharmacology MOC]], but tested as its own domain.

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Physiology of pain: the pain pathway, acute vs chronic, wind-up
- Recognizing pain by species; validated pain scales (for example the Glasgow and Colorado State scales, feline grimace scale)
- Analgesic classes: opioids, NSAIDs, local anesthetics, alpha-2 agonists, ketamine and other adjuncts
- Pre-emptive and multimodal analgesia
- Local and regional blocks; epidurals; constant rate infusions (CRIs)
- Chronic pain and osteoarthritis management; non-drug therapies (rehabilitation, laser, acupuncture)
- Client education and at-home pain monitoring
