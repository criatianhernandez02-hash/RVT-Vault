# Exotic Animal Medicine MOC

**Not a VTNE domain of its own.** Exotic-animal questions show up inside Animal Care and Nursing, Anesthesia and the other domains, and vet tech programs teach exotics as a subject, so they get their own home here.

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Husbandry and nutrition: birds, reptiles and amphibians, rabbits, ferrets, guinea pigs, rodents, fish
- Species-specific anatomy and physiology differences that change nursing care
- Restraint, handling and sample collection in small and fragile patients
- Common diseases and husbandry-related problems (for example metabolic bone disease, GI stasis in rabbits)
- Anesthesia and analgesia considerations for exotics
- Zoonoses and safety
- Laboratory animal and zoo basics
