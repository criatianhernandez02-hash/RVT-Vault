# Topics MOC

The Veterinary Technician National Examination has 150 scored questions across **ten domains**. Organizing your knowledge the same way means that by graduation your vault *is* your VTNE study guide. Each domain below has its own folder and map; each map lists what the domain covers so you can spot gaps.

| Domain                                         | Items | Weight | What it is, in one line                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----: | -----: | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Animal Care and Nursing MOC]]                |    30 |   20 % | Restraint, exams, nursing care, fluids, husbandry         |
| [[Pharmacy and Pharmacology MOC]]              |    20 |   13 % | Drugs, doses, prescriptions, controlled substances        |
| [[Surgical Nursing MOC]]                       |    20 |   13 % | Asepsis, instruments, patient prep, assisting, post-op    |
| [[Anesthesia MOC]]                             |    20 |   13 % | Pre-anesthetic to recovery: drugs, machines, monitoring   |
| [[Laboratory Procedures MOC]]                  |    14 |    9 % | Hematology, chemistry, urinalysis, cytology, parasitology |
| [[Dentistry MOC]]                              |    10 |    7 % | Dental anatomy, charting, prophylaxis, dental radiographs |
| [[Emergency and Critical Care MOC]]            |    10 |    7 % | Triage, CPR, shock, toxicities, critical monitoring       |
| [[Pain Management and Analgesia MOC]]          |    10 |    7 % | Recognizing and scoring pain, analgesics, local blocks    |
| [[Diagnostic Imaging MOC]]                     |     9 |    6 % | Radiography, positioning, radiation safety, ultrasound    |
| [[Communication and Professional Support MOC]] |     7 |    5 % | Client and team communication, ethics, law, records       |

Weights are from the AAVSB's 2023 VTNE Content Outline, the version current exams use ([official PDF](https://www.aavsb.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/AAVSB-2023-Veterinary-Technician-National-Examination-Content-Outline-2.pdf)). The exam has 170 questions, of which 150 are scored; the item counts above are the scored ones, and the 20-per-domain figures for the three 13 % domains are worked out from the outline's totals.

## How to file a topic note
1. Pick the domain the *exam* would file it under, not the course you learned it in.
2. Use the **Topic Note**, **Drug Card**, **Condition Card** or **Procedure Note** template.
3. Set `vtne_domain` in the frontmatter to the domain name exactly as written above.
4. Link the note from its domain MOC, and link back to the lecture where you met it.
