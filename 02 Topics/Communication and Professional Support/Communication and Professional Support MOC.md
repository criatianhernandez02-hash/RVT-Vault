# Communication and Professional Support MOC

**VTNE weight: 5 %.** The official outline calls this domain *Communication and Veterinary Professional Support Services*, and it is the newest domain (added in the 2023 outline). Small, but these are points you can bank: they reward judgment, not memorized numbers.

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Client communication: taking a history, explaining diagnoses and estimates, informed consent, delivering bad news, cultural sensitivity
- Euthanasia conversations and grief support
- Team communication, delegation, conflict resolution, and the technician's scope of practice
- Veterinary ethics and confidentiality; the laws that govern practice (state practice acts, credentialing, controlled-substance rules)
- Medical records: legal requirements, SOAP format, discharge instructions
- Practice operations: inventory and pharmacy management, scheduling, telemedicine basics
- Workplace safety: OSHA, hazard communication, radiation and anesthetic-gas safety programs
- Professional wellbeing: compassion fatigue, burnout, continuing education
