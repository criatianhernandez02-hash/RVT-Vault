# Veterinary Regulatory Agencies

> [!note] Seeded by Claude before you took Lesson 6
> Written from the agencies' own public roles, not from your course. After the lesson, compare and fix, especially anything about your state, then move `status` to `growing`.

> [!abstract] In one sentence
> Government agencies (federal and state) make and enforce the rules; professional associations set standards, accredit programs and run exams; the exam loves to ask which one does what.

## Government agencies

| Agency | Level | What it controls that touches your job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **State Veterinary Medical Board** (in California, the Veterinary Medical Board) | State | Licenses veterinarians and credentials technicians (RVT, LVT or CVT depending on the state); enforces the state **Veterinary Practice Act**, which defines what a technician may do and under what supervision; handles complaints and discipline |
| **DEA** (Drug Enforcement Administration) | Federal | Controlled substances: the five schedules, practice registration, ordering (Form 222 for Schedule II), locked storage, logs and inventories, reporting theft or loss |
| **FDA, Center for Veterinary Medicine (CVM)** | Federal | Approves animal drugs and medicated feeds, controls labels, sets the rules for **extralabel drug use** (AMDUCA), collects adverse drug event reports |
| **USDA, APHIS** (Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service) | Federal | Animal disease control and reportable diseases, import and export, accredits veterinarians to sign health certificates, regulates **vaccines and other biologics** (Center for Veterinary Biologics), enforces the **Animal Welfare Act** |
| **EPA** (Environmental Protection Agency) | Federal | Pesticides, including many **topical flea and tick products** that are not FDA drugs; disposal rules for some chemicals |
| **OSHA** (Occupational Safety and Health Administration) | Federal | Your safety at work: hazard communication and safety data sheets (SDS), PPE, anesthetic waste gas, radiation safety programs, sharps |
| **CDC** (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention) | Federal | Human public health: zoonotic disease guidance, rabies post-exposure recommendations |
| **State Department of Agriculture / State Veterinarian** | State | Livestock disease programs, reportable disease lists, rabies control in many states |

## Professional organizations (not government, but they shape the profession)

| Organization | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **AVMA** (American Veterinary Medical Association) | The veterinarians' professional association. Its **CVTEA** committee accredits veterinary technician programs (Penn Foster's is CVTEA-accredited), it publishes the Model Veterinary Practice Act and position statements, and it sets many professional standards |
| **AAVSB** (American Association of Veterinary State Boards) | The association of the state licensing boards. Writes and administers the **VTNE**, and helps credentials transfer between states |
| **NAVTA** (National Association of Veterinary Technicians in America) | The technicians' professional association: defines the profession, recognizes the **VTS specialty academies**, runs National Veterinary Technician Week, advocates on titles and credentialing |
| **State VMA and state technician associations** | Continuing education, local advocacy, job boards |

## Memory hook
**"Drugs FDA, controlled drugs DEA, vaccines and diseases USDA, bug products EPA, your safety OSHA, your license the state board, your exam AAVSB, your school AVMA."**

## Common mistakes and exam traps
- Vaccines are **USDA**, not FDA. Drugs are FDA. Controlled drugs add the DEA on top of FDA.
- Many spot-on flea products are **EPA**-registered pesticides, while oral flea products are FDA drugs. The label tells you which: an EPA registration number vs an FDA NADA/ANADA number.
- The AVMA does not license anyone; **your state board** does.
- The VTNE is run by the **AAVSB**, not by the AVMA or NAVTA.

## Flashcards
#flashcards/communication

Which agency regulates veterinary vaccines and biologics?::USDA APHIS, through its Center for Veterinary Biologics.

Which agency accredits veterinary technician programs?::The AVMA, through its Committee on Veterinary Technician Education and Activities (CVTEA).

Who administers the VTNE?::The American Association of Veterinary State Boards (AAVSB).

Which agency sets the scheduling, storage and record-keeping rules for controlled substances?::The DEA.

Which federal agency covers workplace safety in a veterinary clinic, such as hazard communication and PPE?::OSHA.
