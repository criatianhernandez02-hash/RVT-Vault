# Pharmacy and Pharmacology MOC

**VTNE weight: 13 %.** Every drug you meet gets a **Drug Card** (template in `Templates`), filed in this folder and linked below.

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Major drug classes and how they work: antimicrobials, anti-inflammatories (NSAIDs, steroids), analgesics, anesthetics and sedatives, cardiovascular, GI, endocrine, antiparasitics, vaccines and biologics
- Dose calculations, conversions, percent solutions ([[Drug Dosage Calculations]])
- Reading and filling prescriptions, labeling, dispensing law
- Controlled substances: DEA schedules, logs, storage, handling
- Routes of administration and how they change onset and duration
- Adverse reactions, interactions, contraindications, toxicities
- Pharmacokinetics basics: absorption, distribution, metabolism, excretion
- Compounding, storage and expiration, client education
