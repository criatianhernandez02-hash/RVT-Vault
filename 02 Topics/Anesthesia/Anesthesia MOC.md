# Anesthesia MOC

**VTNE weight: 13 %.** Technicians run anesthesia in most practices, so this domain is tested in depth.

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Pre-anesthetic assessment, bloodwork, ASA physical status classification
- Pre-medication, induction and maintenance drugs; reversal agents
- The anesthetic machine: gas supply, flowmeters, vaporizers, breathing circuits, scavenging, leak checks
- Endotracheal intubation and airway management
- Monitoring: heart rate and ECG, pulse oximetry, capnography, blood pressure, temperature, reflexes and anesthetic depth
- Stages and planes of anesthesia
- Fluid support and patient warming during anesthesia
- Complications and emergencies: hypotension, hypoventilation, hypothermia, arrhythmias, cardiac arrest
- Recovery and extubation; species and breed considerations
- Record keeping and the anesthetic log
