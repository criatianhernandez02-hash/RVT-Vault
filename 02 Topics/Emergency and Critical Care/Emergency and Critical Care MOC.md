# Emergency and Critical Care MOC

**VTNE weight: 7 %** (listed in the outline as Emergency Medicine / Critical Care).

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Triage and the primary survey; telephone triage
- CPR and the RECOVER guidelines; crash cart contents and emergency drugs
- Shock: types, recognition, treatment
- Fluid resuscitation and blood transfusions
- Oxygen therapy and respiratory distress
- Toxicities and poisonings: common toxins, decontamination, antidotes
- Trauma, hemorrhage, wounds, burns, heat stroke, hypothermia
- Common emergencies: GDV (bloat), urethral obstruction ("blocked cat"), dystocia, seizures, diabetic crises
- Critical patient monitoring and nursing in the ICU
