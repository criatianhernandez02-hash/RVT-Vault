# Surgical Nursing MOC

**VTNE weight: 13 %.**

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Aseptic technique and the sterile field; surgical attire, scrubbing, gowning, gloving
- Sterilization and disinfection: autoclave, indicators, pack wrapping, instrument care
- Surgical instruments: identification, use, passing
- Patient preparation: fasting, clipping, surgical scrub, positioning, draping
- Suture materials, needles and patterns; staples and tissue adhesives
- Surgical assisting and circulating nurse duties
- Common procedures and what the tech does in each: spay (OHE), castration, laparotomy, cystotomy, orthopedic repairs, mass removals
- Post-operative care: recovery, pain assessment, incision care, bandages, drains, complications
- Operating room maintenance and safety
