# Animal Care and Nursing MOC

**VTNE weight: 20 %, the biggest single domain.** This is the day-to-day job of a technician, so expect the exam to lean on it heavily.

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Restraint and handling, low-stress techniques, species differences
- History taking, physical examination, TPR, body condition scoring ([[Normal Values]])
- Medical records, SOAP notes, legal and ethical responsibilities
- Fluid therapy: catheter placement, rates, monitoring ([[Drug Dosage Calculations]])
- Medication administration by every route
- Nutrition, feeding tubes, therapeutic diets
- Bandaging, splinting, wound care
- Hospitalized and recumbent patient care, nursing care plans
- Preventive medicine: vaccines, parasite control, wellness
- Large animal and farm animal husbandry and nursing
- Zoonoses, biosecurity, infection control, sanitation
- Euthanasia, grief support, client communication and education
