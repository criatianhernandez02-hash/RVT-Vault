# Laboratory Procedures MOC

**VTNE weight: 9 %.**

## Typical coverage (use as a gap checklist)
- Sample collection, handling, anticoagulant tubes, storage and shipping
- Hematology: CBC, blood smear and differential, PCV and total protein, reticulocytes ([[Normal Values]])
- Clinical chemistry panels and what each value means
- Urinalysis: collection methods, specific gravity, dipstick, sediment
- Cytology: ear swabs, skin scrapings, fine-needle aspirates, fluid analysis
- Parasitology: fecal flotation and direct smears, heartworm testing, identifying common parasites
- Microbiology: culture, Gram stain, sensitivity testing
- Blood typing and cross-matching
- Quality control, lab safety, equipment maintenance, necropsy basics
