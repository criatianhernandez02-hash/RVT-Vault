# Semester 1 Pacing Plan

> [!abstract] The deal
> Penn Foster's 6-month guide runs **Sun Nov 1, 2026 → Fri Apr 30, 2027**: 37 sections across six courses, one every 4.9 days. You started **Mon Nov 9, 2026** and want 4–5 months. This plan does **two sections a week** (targets on Wednesday and Saturday), takes Christmas week off for catch-up, and finishes on **Wed Mar 24, 2027**: 135 days, about 4.4 months, leaving **37 days of cushion** before Penn Foster's own end date. Their dates are the backstop, not the target.

## The daily rhythm (1 to 2 hours)
The full method for working through a section is in [[How to Study a Section]]; this is how it fits into a night.
1. **Open today's study log** (`Ctrl/Cmd + P` → *Open today's daily note*) and write down the one section you're on. 2 minutes.
2. **Flashcards, 10 minutes.** Yesterday's cards plus one older set. With the Spaced Repetition plugin this is automatic; without it, open a set and cover the answers.
3. **Lesson work, 45 to 90 minutes.** Read with the section's note open. Notes in your own words, one idea per bullet; key terms as you meet them; anything confusing goes under *Questions I still have*.
4. **Close out, 5 minutes.** On the last day of a section, write the three-sentence summary and at least five flashcards. Update the log. If auto-sync hasn't run, `Ctrl/Cmd + P` → *Git: Commit-and-sync*.

One hour is the floor, even on a bad day (flashcards plus reading counts). Two hours is the ceiling on work nights: protect sleep, the material is cumulative.

## The weekly rhythm
| Day | Plan |
| --- | --- |
| Monday, Tuesday | Read and take notes for section A |
| **Wednesday** | 10 minutes of review, then section A's exam. **Target day.** |
| Thursday, Friday | Read and take notes for section B |
| **Saturday** | Review, section B's exam, then ask Claude *"summarize my week"*. **Target day.** |
| Sunday | Off. Or catch-up. Or 30 minutes of old flashcards if you feel like it. |

## Rules
- Take a section's exam only when its note has a summary and at least five flashcards.
- Never start the next section with the current one unfinished, and never skip an exam.
- **Saturday check:** compare with the list below. One section behind: use Sunday. More than a week behind at the end of a course: tell Claude *"re-plan"* and the dates get recomputed from where you are, still two a week, never past Fri Apr 30, 2027.
- Before every capstone or final: *"find gaps in <course>"* and *"quiz me on <course>, twenty questions"*.
- When you pass a section's exam, set that note's `status` to `evergreen` and tick it below.

## Live view
These two lists are powered by the **Dataview** plugin, which ships with the vault.

**Due in the next seven days**
```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID file.link AS "Section", course AS "Course", target AS "Target"
FROM "01 Courses"
WHERE type = "lecture" AND target AND target >= date(today) AND target <= date(today) + dur(7 days)
SORT target ASC
```

**Behind** (target passed, not yet marked `evergreen`)
```dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID file.link AS "Section", target AS "Target"
FROM "01 Courses"
WHERE type = "lecture" AND target AND target < date(today) AND status != "evergreen"
SORT target ASC
```
