# Courses MOC

Penn Foster's Veterinary Technician A.S. is four semesters, 66 credits, with Externship 1 at the end of semester 2 and Externship 2 at the end of semester 4. Semester 1 below comes straight from your pacing guide; later semesters get filled in when you reach them.

## How courses are organized
- One folder per course inside `01 Courses`, each with its own `<Course> MOC` note linking every section.
- Section notes use the **Lecture Note** template and are named `<Code> L<nn> - <Title>`, for example `IVT L03 - Professional Behavior in the Veterinary Field`. The code keeps names unique across the vault (Obsidian links by filename) and `L<nn>` keeps them in order.
- Facts that outlive the course belong in `02 Topics`, linked from the section note. The section note is *where you learned it*; the topic note is *what you know*.
- Dates and the study rhythm live in [[Semester 1 Pacing Plan]].
